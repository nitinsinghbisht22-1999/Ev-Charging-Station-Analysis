EV Charging Station Analytics

Project Overview

This project is a complete business intelligence solution developed in Power BI to analyze the operational, financial, and customer performance of an EV charging station company operating across multiple cities in India.

The goal of the project is to help management identify:

Why customers are churning
What impacts customer satisfaction
Which stations and cities perform poorly
How maintenance costs affect revenue
Which cities offer strong expansion opportunities

The dashboards provide actionable insights using customer analytics, operational KPIs, maintenance analysis, and strategic business recommendations.

Business Problem:

As EV adoption increases, the company faces multiple operational and customer experience challenges:

Rising customer churn
Increasing maintenance costs
Poor customer satisfaction in certain cities
Network downtime and charging issues
Uneven infrastructure distribution

Management wants to leverage analytics to improve:

Customer retention
Charging network performance
Operational efficiency
Revenue optimization
Expansion planning
Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

Analyze customer churn patterns
Identify key drivers of customer dissatisfaction
Monitor charging network operational issues
Compare maintenance costs against generated revenue
Evaluate city-wise expansion opportunities
Build an executive-level Power BI reporting solution
Dashboards Included


1. Executive Overview

Provides:

Business problem statement
Project objectives
Strategic overview
Dashboard navigation


2. Customer Retention Analytics

Analyzes:

Customer churn trends
Churn reasons
Churn by subscription type
Churn across cities
Charger types used by churned customers
Key Insights
Slow charging was the primary reason for customer churn
Enterprise users recorded the highest churn
Hyderabad and Pune had high churn counts


3. Charging Network Performance

Analyzes:

Charging network operational issues
Station-wise issue tracking
City-wise issue distribution
Issue trends over time
Key Insights
Power outages and port failures were major operational problems
Bangalore recorded the highest issue count
Network reliability requires improvement


4. Customer Experience Analytics

Analyzes:

Complaint types
Customer feedback trends
Average customer ratings
Negative feedback by city
Revenue by energy consumption behavior
Key Insights
Slow charging significantly reduced customer satisfaction
Bangalore and Pune received high negative feedback
High energy consumption customers generated higher revenue


5. Maintenance vs Revenue Analysis

Analyzes:

Revenue versus maintenance costs
Station profitability
City-level operational costs
Revenue impact by customer activity
Maintenance trends over time
Key Insights
Active customers generated significantly higher revenue
Ultra Fast chargers generated high revenue but incurred high maintenance costs
Maintenance expenses increased steadily from 2023 to 2025


6. Expansion Strategy Dashboard

Analyzes:

Existing charging infrastructure
User distribution across cities
Customer churn risk
Customer dissatisfaction
Revenue and maintenance comparison by city
Key Insights
Chennai and Bangalore showed strong infrastructure presence
Bangalore and Pune displayed high dissatisfaction and churn risk
Hyderabad had lower market penetration

Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

Improve charging speed reliability to reduce customer churn
Reduce downtime through preventive maintenance
Optimize maintenance spending on high-cost stations
Expand infrastructure in cities with high demand and lower dissatisfaction
Improve queue management to enhance customer satisfaction
